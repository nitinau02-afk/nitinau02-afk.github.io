02 — Research

Questions I want to answer.

Algorithmic Management & the Employment Relationship

My research explores how algorithmic management and AI-enabled HR systems reshape the relationship between employees and organisations. The focus is on how employees interpret technology-driven decisions, how trust develops or deteriorates, and how psychological contracts are reconfigured when organisational decisions increasingly involve algorithmic systems.
