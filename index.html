About

People, organisations and technology

My research focuses on how emerging technologies are influencing the relationship between employees and organisations.

I am particularly interested in the growing use of artificial intelligence in Human Resource Management and the emergence of algorithmic forms of organisational decision-making.

Across my work, I examine questions of employee trust, psychological contracts, organisational justice, employee voice, accountability and responsible AI.

Research

Current research direction

Algorithmic Management and the Employment Relationship: Employee Trust and Psychological Contract Reconfiguration in AI-enabled HRM

This research explores how AI-mediated organisational decisions may reshape employee perceptions of authority, fairness, responsibility and mutual obligations within the employment relationship.
