About

My research sits at the intersection of people, technology and organisations.

I am an early-career researcher in Human Resource Management and Organisational Behaviour, with a particular interest in artificial intelligence, algorithmic management and the future of work.

My work examines how AI-mediated organisational decision-making influences employee trust, psychological contracts, organisational justice, employee voice and employment relationships.

I am particularly interested in questions of authority, responsibility, fairness and accountability when organisational decisions increasingly involve algorithmic systems.
