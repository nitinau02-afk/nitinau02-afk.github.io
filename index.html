Research

Where technology
meets the employment
relationship.

My work focuses on what happens to trust, responsibility and fairness when organisational decisions become increasingly mediated by artificial intelligence.

Central question

How does algorithmic decision-making reshape what employees expect from organisations — and what organisations expect from employees?

My emerging doctoral research agenda examines AI-mediated organisational decision-making and its implications for the employment relationship.

I am particularly interested in how employees understand authority, responsibility, fairness, accountability and mutual obligations when technology becomes part of organisational decision-making.

This brings together Human Resource Management, Organisational Behaviour, employment relations and responsible AI.
