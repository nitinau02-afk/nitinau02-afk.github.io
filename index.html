About

Research interests and academic work

I am an early-career researcher working at the intersection of Human Resource Management, Organisational Behaviour and technology-enabled organisational change.

My current research focuses on how artificial intelligence and algorithmic systems are changing organisational decision-making, authority, responsibility and the employment relationship.

I am particularly interested in the implications of these technologies for employee trust, psychological contracts, organisational justice, employee voice and responsible implementation of AI in the workplace.

My work combines quantitative, qualitative and mixed-method approaches, with an emphasis on developing theoretically grounded and empirically testable explanations of technology-mediated workplace relationships.

Research

Current research direction

Algorithmic Management and the Employment Relationship: Employee Trust and Psychological Contract Reconfiguration in AI-enabled HRM

This research examines how AI-mediated organisational decision-making can reshape the employment relationship. It focuses on how employees interpret algorithmic authority, fairness, accountability and responsibility, and how these perceptions influence trust and expectations between employees and organisations.

AI & HRM

Examining the adoption and organisational consequences of artificial intelligence in recruitment and broader HR practices.

Algorithmic Management

Investigating how algorithmic systems influence managerial authority, decision-making, control and employee experiences.

Trust & Psychological Contracts

Understanding how technology-mediated work changes employee expectations, perceived obligations and organisational trust.

Organisational Justice

Exploring employee perceptions of fairness, transparency, accountability and legitimacy in technology-enabled decisions.

Employee Voice

Studying how digital and algorithmic systems affect employee participation, voice and opportunities to influence decisions.

Responsible AI

Considering the organisational and human implications of responsible, transparent and accountable AI implementation.
