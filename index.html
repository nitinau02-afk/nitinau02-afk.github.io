About

Technology is changing work. I want to understand what that means for people.


My research sits at the intersection of Human Resource Management, Organisational Behaviour and emerging technologies. I am particularly interested in how artificial intelligence and algorithmic systems influence employees and organisations.

My work explores questions of trust, fairness, responsibility, employee voice and mutual obligations when organisational decisions increasingly involve AI-enabled systems.

I approach these questions through empirical research, combining quantitative, qualitative and mixed-method approaches to understand both organisational systems and employee experiences.

Research

What I study

My current research agenda examines the changing relationship between employees, organisations and algorithmic systems.

Algorithmic Management and the Employment Relationship

My current research investigates how algorithmic management reconfigures the employment relationship in AI-enabled organisations. The project focuses particularly on employee trust and the psychological contract — examining how employees understand organisational obligations, fairness, authority and responsibility when decisions are increasingly mediated by artificial intelligence.
