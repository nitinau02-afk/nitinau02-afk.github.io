About

I study how technology is changing the relationship between people and organisations.

I am an early-career researcher in Human Resource Management and Organisational Behaviour, with a particular interest in artificial intelligence, algorithmic management and the future of work.

My work examines how AI-enabled organisational decision-making influences employee trust, psychological contracts, organisational justice, employee voice and employment relationships.

A central interest in my research is what happens to authority, responsibility, fairness and accountability when organisational decisions become increasingly mediated by algorithmic systems.

Research

Understanding work in an increasingly algorithmic workplace.

My current research direction examines how algorithmic management and AI-enabled HR practices reshape employee perceptions of trust, fairness and mutual obligations at work.
