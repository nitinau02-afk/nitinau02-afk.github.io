About

I am an early-career researcher working at the intersection of Human Resource Management, Organisational Behaviour, employment relationships and emerging technologies.

My research focuses particularly on AI-enabled HRM, algorithmic management, psychological contracts, employee trust, organisational justice, employee voice and responsible AI in the workplace.

I am interested in understanding how AI-mediated organisational decision-making changes employees' perceptions of authority, fairness, responsibility, accountability and mutual obligations within the employment relationship.

My methodological interests include quantitative research, mixed methods, structural equation modelling, scale development and validation, qualitative research and computational approaches to organisational research.

Current Research Direction

Psychological Contract Reconfiguration in AI-Governed Organisations

Employee Trust and Responsible AI

This research investigates how employees experience and interpret AI-mediated HR decisions and how the increasing involvement of AI in organisational decision-making may reshape psychological contracts, employee trust, perceptions of fairness, responsibility, accountability and workplace well-being.

The proposed research uses a phased mixed-methods design combining qualitative interviews, scale development, longitudinal quantitative research and multilevel analysis.
