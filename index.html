About Me

Research interests and academic profile

I am an early-career researcher in Human Resource Management and Organisational Behaviour, with research interests at the intersection of AI-enabled HRM, algorithmic management, employment relationships and organisational behaviour.

My research examines how AI-enabled HR decision-making and algorithmic management can reshape organisational authority, fairness, accountability, employee trust and psychological contracts.

My methodological interests include quantitative and mixed-method research, survey research, structural equation modelling, psychometrics, scale development and qualitative research.

Current Research Direction

Algorithmic Management and the Employment Relationship: Employee Trust and Psychological Contract Reconfiguration in AI-enabled HRM

My emerging doctoral research agenda examines how AI-mediated organisational decision-making may alter employees' understanding of authority, responsibility, fairness, accountability and mutual obligations within the employment relationship.

  • AI-enabled HRM and algorithmic management as organisational and employment transformations.
  • Employee trust, organisational justice and transparency.
  • Psychological contract reconfiguration in AI-mediated work.
  • Employee voice, human oversight and responsible AI governance.
