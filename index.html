About

Researching the future of work.

My work examines how emerging technologies are changing employment relationships, organisational practices and employee experiences.

Research Profile


I am an early-career researcher working across Human Resource Management, Organisational Behaviour and Algorithmic Management. My research focuses on how artificial intelligence influences organisational decision-making, employee trust, psychological contracts, fairness and employee voice.

AI-enabled HRM Algorithmic Management Employee Trust Psychological Contracts Organisational Justice Employee Voice Responsible AI Future of Work

Research Philosophy


I am particularly interested in understanding not only whether organisations adopt AI, but how employees experience and interpret technology-mediated changes in authority, responsibility, fairness and mutual obligations.


My broader research agenda seeks to contribute to a more human-centred understanding of digital transformation within organisations.

Research Programme

Algorithmic Management and the Employment Relationship: Employee Trust and Psychological Contract Reconfiguration in AI-enabled HRM

This research direction investigates how the growing use of AI in organisational decision-making may influence employee trust, perceived organisational obligations, psychological contracts and perceptions of fairness.

Core question How does algorithmic management reshape the employment relationship and employees' perceptions of trust, fairness and mutual obligations?
