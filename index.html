About me

I study the relationship between people, organisations and emerging technologies.

I am an early-career researcher in Human Resource Management and Organisational Behaviour, with a particular interest in artificial intelligence, algorithmic management and the future of work.

My work examines how AI-mediated organisational decision-making influences employee trust, psychological contracts, organisational justice, employee voice and employment relationships.

A central interest in my research is what happens to authority, responsibility, fairness and accountability when decisions traditionally made by people become increasingly mediated by algorithmic systems.

Research

Understanding work in an increasingly algorithmic workplace.

My current research direction examines how algorithmic management and AI-enabled HR practices reshape the employment relationship and employee perceptions of organisational decision-making.

Algorithmic Management and the Employment Relationship: Employee Trust and Psychological Contract Reconfiguration in AI-enabled HRM

The research considers how AI-mediated decisions may influence perceptions of trust, fairness, authority, responsibility and mutual obligations between employees and organisations.
