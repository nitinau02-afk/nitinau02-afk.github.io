About

Technology may change the way work is organised — but people remain at the centre.


My work sits at the intersection of Human Resource Management, Organisational Behaviour and emerging technologies.

I am particularly interested in what happens when artificial intelligence moves beyond being a tool and becomes part of organisational decision-making.

This leads me to questions about trust, fairness, accountability, employee voice, psychological contracts and the changing relationship between employees and organisations.

Current research direction

Algorithmic Management and the Employment Relationship

Employee trust and psychological contract reconfiguration in AI-enabled HRM. The project examines how employees interpret organisational authority, responsibility and fairness when decisions are increasingly mediated by algorithmic systems.
