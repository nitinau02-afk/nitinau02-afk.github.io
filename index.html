About

My research examines the changing relationship between employees, organisations and technology.

I am an early-career researcher in Human Resource Management and Organisational Behaviour, with a particular interest in artificial intelligence, algorithmic management and the future of work.

My work examines how AI-enabled organisational decision-making influences employee trust, psychological contracts, organisational justice, employee voice and employment relationships.

I am particularly interested in questions of authority, responsibility, fairness and accountability when organisational decisions increasingly involve algorithmic systems.

Research

My current research focuses on how algorithmic management and AI-enabled HR practices may reshape employee perceptions of trust, fairness and mutual obligations at work.

Algorithmic Management and the Employment Relationship: Employee Trust and Psychological Contract Reconfiguration in AI-enabled HRM

The research considers how AI-mediated organisational decisions may change perceptions of authority, responsibility, fairness and accountability within the employment relationship.
