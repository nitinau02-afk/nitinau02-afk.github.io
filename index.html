About

Technology changes work. People give it meaning.


My work sits at the intersection of Human Resource Management, Organisational Behaviour and emerging technologies.

I am interested in what happens when artificial intelligence moves beyond being a tool and becomes part of organisational decision-making.

This leads me to questions about employee trust, fairness, accountability, employee voice, psychological contracts and the changing relationship between employees and organisations.

Current research direction

Algorithmic Management and the Employment Relationship

Employee trust and psychological contract reconfiguration in AI-enabled HRM. I examine how employees understand authority, responsibility and fairness when organisational decisions increasingly involve algorithmic systems.
