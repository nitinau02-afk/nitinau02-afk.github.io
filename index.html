Research

Understanding work
in an AI-enabled world.

Research interests at the intersection of people, organisations and emerging technologies.

I am interested in how AI and algorithmic systems influence the way employees understand trust, fairness, responsibility and their relationship with organisations.

My emerging research agenda examines AI-mediated organisational decision-making and its implications for the employment relationship.

I am particularly interested in authority, accountability, transparency, employee voice, organisational justice and the reconfiguration of psychological contracts in AI-mediated work.
